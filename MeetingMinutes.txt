Questions to ask in the first 30 (wont ask all of these but are good prompts if I get stuck in the convo lol):

- Request a data sample
- What is the aim of the project
- What do they need out of me
- What is the scope of the project?
- Project requirements
	- What is the end goal of the analytics
	- What types of analytics are they looking for
	- What do they want the model to be able to predict
	- What are the key aspects of the prediction they want me to focus on (is reliable long term time dependence a higher priority to short term time dependence)
	- What do you want predicted, what do you want this prediction to be based off of
- What does the data itself look like
	- Is it time dependent
	- Is there a periodic nature to it
	- How many parameters are we looking at
- Is there anything in particular you don't want done with the data
	- Are there any goals you want ignored
	- Is there any data you would consider irrelevant
