conversion to grid (~the shape of the uk)
- how to implement
	- access the grid through an x and y value, keeps most of the spatial dependence while helping produce a balanced dataset
		- new problem: the UK is NOT rectangular
	- calculations
	- weighting
	- how big do I do the grids? UK is 244376 km^2
		- leaning 1km x 1km but that is LOTS of data for a single day and I want to look at years
		- maybe 5x5 km^2 (25km^2)??????
	- with land cover, just pick the most prevalent for the area (don't want to overcomplicate this)
	- with pop density, use an average where required
	- climate data, average where needed
	- may require smoothing depending on data resolution
	- wildfire data, maybe weight based off of confidence level, look into reasonable ways of doing that 
		- want high confidences to carry more weight than low confidences
- pros
	- makes balanced dataset easier
	- makes assigning other data factors easier
	- makes assigning regional risk factors easier
	- preserves spatial relationships while simplifying them
	- mimics how map grid coords work on OS maps
	- geopandas comes with a built in way to do this
		- https://james-brennan.github.io/posts/fast_gridding_geopandas/
- cons
	- adds SO MANY datapoints that we don't really need, could 'flood out' the wildfires
		- especially in a less prone area like the UK
	- could cause problems with convolutional models as its not going to be an even grid without adding Even More data, essentially removes that option
